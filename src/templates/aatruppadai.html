The idea

More than giving directions

In ancient Tamil Sangam literature, ஆற்றுப்படை was an act of guiding someone towards a better destination: sharing knowledge, pointing out opportunities, naming generous people and making sure no traveller felt lost. In poems such as the பெரும்பாணாற்றுப்படை (Perumpanatruppadai), a bard who has been well received tells a fellow traveller the road, where to eat and who will welcome him.
