      Getting around

      Government-approved cabs and rentals

      Ride only in vehicles the government permits for hire. Roots will list cab operators only after checking their permit, driver badge and insurance.

      Airport prepaid taxi

      At the counter in the Madurai Airport arrivals hall you pay a fixed fare before you leave, and the receipt names the driver and vehicle. Expect roughly ₹400–600 to the temple area; the counter's fare chart is final.

      Directions

      TTDC tours and coaches

      Tamil Nadu Tourism Development Corporation, the state's own tourism company, runs package tours and arranges coaches for groups on request. Its Madurai base is Hotel Tamil Nadu (Unit 1, West Veli Street; Unit 2, Alagar Kovil Road).

      Tamil Nadu Tourism toll-free: 1800 425 31111

      TNSTC city buses

      The state transport corporation runs Madurai's city buses from Periyar, Mattuthavani and Arappalayam bus stands, including a stop at the airport. The cheapest way to reach the temple and markets. Every place on Roots has a Bus route from my location button.

      Self-drive rentals

      Only cars under the government Rent-a-Cab scheme may be rented for self-drive. They carry a black number plate with yellow letters. A private white-plate car rented out is illegal and its insurance won't cover you.

      Is this cab legal? Check before you sit in

      • Yellow plate, black letters: a taxi with a commercial permit.
      • Black plate, yellow letters: a licensed self-drive rental.
      • White plate: a private car. Don't hire it.
      • Outstation trips: ask to see the tourist permit and the driver's badge.
      • Use the airport prepaid counter or a well-known app or operator. Note the vehicle number before you start.

      Run a permitted taxi or rental in Madurai? List it on Roots. We check the permit, badge, insurance and fitness certificate before listing.
