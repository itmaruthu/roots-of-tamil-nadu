      Live wait · Aatruppadai

      Know the crowd before you go

      No app can make a famous temple or mess less crowded, and nobody should jump the line. What Roots can do is tell you how full a place is before you leave your room, and let a family ask for a table at a set time. It needs no TV, no machine and no app: only the owner's own phone.

      1. See the crowd before you goEvery eatery shows Seats free, Filling up or House full. The owner sets it with one tap, or a WhatsApp reply; visitors' reports fill the gaps.
      2. House full? Go later or go nearbyRoots shows when the rush usually eases today and which place close by has seats right now.
      3. Coming as a family? Ask for a tablePick the time and the number of people. The request goes to the owner's own WhatsApp as a ready-written message. The owner replies yes, another time or no. A yes is held for 10 minutes.

      Try it: two phones, nothing else

      Set the crowd as the owner and watch the guest's phone change. Then ask for a family table as the guest: it opens a real WhatsApp message to the owner's phone, which for this demo is 63804 65605 for every eatery.

        Chithirai Thiruvizha · சித்திரைத் திருவிழா · April–May

        The wedding of a goddess, and a god who comes too late

        Madurai's greatest festival fills about two weeks of the Tamil month of Chithirai. Meenakshi is crowned and married at her temple; then her brother Kallazhagar rides down from the Alagar hills and enters the Vaigai before lakhs of people. Two festivals, Shaiva and Vaishnava, joined into one for the whole city.

        1. Kodiyetram

          கொடியேற்றம்

          The flag is raised at the Meenakshi temple and the festival begins. Each evening the goddess and Sundareswarar ride the Masi streets on a different mount.

        2. Pattabhishekam

          பட்டாபிஷேகம்

          Meenakshi is crowned queen of Madurai and given the sceptre.

        3. Dig Vijayam

          திக்விஜயம்

          The queen rides out to conquer the eight directions, and meets Shiva.

        4. Thirukalyanam

          திருக்கல்யாணம்

          The wedding of Meenakshi and Sundareswarar. Across the city, married women renew their thali thread that morning.

        5. Ther Thiruvizha

          தேரோட்டம்

          Thousands pull the great wooden chariots round the four Masi streets.

        6. Ethir Sevai

          எதிர்சேவை

          Kallazhagar comes down from Alagar Kovil, and the city goes out to Tallakulam at night to welcome him.

        7. Kallazhagar enters the Vaigai

          அழகர் ஆற்றில் இறங்குதல்

          At dawn he rides a golden horse into the river. He was coming to his sister's wedding, the story goes, and learned on the bank that it was over. The colour of his silk robe is read as a sign for the year.

        Come home for Chithirai. Roots plans the festival for families: an Ooru Nanbar who knows where to stand for the chariot and the river, a stay booked months ahead, and tokens at the messes when the city is at its fullest. Dates follow the Tamil calendar and are announced by the temple each year. April is hot, so carry water and see Health & safety.

        Pongal in the villages · mid-January

        Four days when every village celebrates

        Pongal is Tamil Nadu's harvest festival, and it is lived in the villages, not watched from a stage. Around 13–16 January, step into any village around Madurai and you are a guest.

        1. Bhogi

          போகி

          Homes are cleaned and old things burnt in bonfires at dawn to welcome the new.

        2. Thai Pongal

          தைப் பொங்கல்

          Rice and jaggery are cooked in new pots in front of every home. When it boils over, everyone shouts "Pongalo Pongal!" Kolams cover the streets, and Avaniyapuram holds the first jallikattu.

        3. Mattu Pongal

          மாட்டுப் பொங்கல்

          The day of the cattle: bulls and cows are bathed, horns painted and garlanded. Palamedu holds its jallikattu.

        4. Kaanum Pongal

          காணும் பொங்கல்

          The day for visiting: families go out together, meet relatives and play village games. Alanganallur's famous jallikattu follows.

        Celebrate Pongal with a village family. In the Madurai pilot, Roots will match visitors with village host families for Pongal: cook the pongal, draw the kolam, decorate the cattle and go to jallikattu with locals.

        Getting around

        Government-approved cabs and rentals

        Ride only in vehicles the government permits for hire. Roots will list cab operators only after checking their permit, driver badge and insurance.

        Airport prepaid taxi

        At the counter in the Madurai Airport arrivals hall you pay a fixed fare before you leave, and the receipt names the driver and vehicle. Expect roughly ₹400–600 to the temple area; the counter's fare chart is final.

        Directions

        TTDC tours and coaches

        Tamil Nadu Tourism Development Corporation, the state's own tourism company, runs package tours and arranges coaches for groups on request. Its Madurai base is Hotel Tamil Nadu (Unit 1, West Veli Street; Unit 2, Alagar Kovil Road).

        Tamil Nadu Tourism toll-free: 1800 425 31111

        TNSTC city buses

        The state transport corporation runs Madurai's city buses from Periyar, Mattuthavani and Arappalayam bus stands, including a stop at the airport. The cheapest way to reach the temple and markets. Every place on Roots has a Bus route from my location button.

        Self-drive rentals

        Only cars under the government Rent-a-Cab scheme may be rented for self-drive. They carry a black number plate with yellow letters. A private white-plate car rented out is illegal and its insurance won't cover you.

        Is this cab legal? Check before you sit in

        • Yellow plate, black letters: a taxi with a commercial permit.
        • Black plate, yellow letters: a licensed self-drive rental.
        • White plate: a private car. Don't hire it.
        • Outstation trips: ask to see the tourist permit and the driver's badge.
        • Use the airport prepaid counter or a well-known app or operator. Note the vehicle number before you start.

        Run a permitted taxi or rental in Madurai? List it on Roots. We check the permit, badge, insurance and fitness certificate before listing.
